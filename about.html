About

I'm Khadija Basit, a BBIT student. I check whether the information I'm given actually holds up, then turn it into one clear recommendation.

When I get a topic, I start by pulling whatever data or claims exist around it. Then I check each one myself instead of trusting it as is — comparing numbers, testing sources, looking for what actually holds up. Once I know what's real, I narrow it down to one clear next step, not a list of options.

I don't trust a number or a claim just because someone says it's true. I check it myself before it goes into any recommendation.

I'm looking for a chance to do this kind of work for a real team.
