Finding niche marketing channels FlyRank isn't using yet

FlyRank runs on three channels: Meta ads, LinkedIn, and cold email. Their audience — founders, marketers, e-commerce operators, agency operators — is also active in places FlyRank hasn't touched. I found five, and checked each one myself before recommending it.

The problem

Channel lists are easy to find online. Most of them are guesses — no one checks whether the community is still active, still growing, or still full of the right people. I didn't want to hand over a list like that.

How I verified each channel

For every channel below, I checked it directly: current member or visitor counts, how recently people were posting, and whether the audience matched FlyRank's target group. A channel only made the list if it passed all three checks.

The five channels

1. r/Shopify (Reddit)

72K visitors · 364.2K+ followers · Active — last post under 24 hours

Shopify store owners and e-commerce founders talk directly about growth, traffic, and SEO problems here — FlyRank's core audience, unfiltered.

Cost & effort: Free, low effort — one genuine, helpful post or comment (30–45 min).

Test: Share a free SEO tip as a post, no sales pitch, and track engagement.

2. The Marketing Millennials Newsletter

153K+ marketers · Run by Daniel Murray · Highly trusted in the industry

A newsletter, community, and podcast for marketers with an audience that already trusts the source.

Cost & effort: Paid — sponsorship slot, typically $1,000–5,000+ (see their media kit). Medium effort — pitching and preparing ad content.

Test: One small sponsored mention in a single issue, then track response.

3. r/ecommerce (Reddit)

64K visitors

A large, general subreddit for e-commerce operators and founders.

Cost & effort: Free, low effort — one comment or post.

Test: Share a short case study post and check the response.

4. SEO Content AI Discord

5,687 members · Focused on AI-driven SEO/content, GEO/AEO

A Discord community where the audience is already talking about AI-powered SEO and generative-engine optimization.

Cost & effort: Free, medium effort — join and give value in discussions before mentioning FlyRank.

Test: Give one genuinely helpful answer to a GEO/AEO question, then observe the response.

5. Voices of Search (Podcast)

Daily SEO and content marketing interview show · Actively invites outside guests

Followed by SEO professionals and agency operators for authority content.

Cost & effort: Free to appear, high effort — pitching the host, scheduling, recording.

Test: Pitch a FlyRank expert as a guest on GEO/AEO trends or the future of AI search.

What's still unproven

This shortlist tells you where to look and what to test. It doesn't yet tell you how fast each channel would pay off — that's the next thing to measure once a test runs.
